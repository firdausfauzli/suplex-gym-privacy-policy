Privacy Policy

Suplex Gym

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Suplex Gym collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our gym membership and entry application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect your name, mobile phone number, email address, membership details, gym entry records, payment status, and information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, administer memberships, verify gym entry eligibility, record entry and exit activity, process or verify payments, provide customer support, maintain security, and operate the service.

3. Payments

Payments may be handled through ToyyibPay or its payment services. Payment information submitted through a payment provider is subject to that provider's privacy policy and terms. Suplex Gym uses relevant payment status and transaction information to manage memberships and payment records.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with service providers that support hosting, database operations, payment processing, gym access control, and service notifications. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the security of the service.

5. Storage and Security

Information is stored using systems needed to operate the service. We take reasonable measures to protect it against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage accounts, memberships, entry records, payments, security, and legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your account or to request correction or deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children's Privacy

Suplex Gym is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Suplex Gym service operator through the official contact details provided by the gym.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Suplex Gym app and related membership and gym entry services.
