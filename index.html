Privacy Policy

Suplex Gym

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Suplex Gym handles information used by its gym membership and entry service.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, mobile number, email address, membership details, gym entry records and payment status.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to manage accounts and memberships, verify entry eligibility, record gym entry and exit, manage payments, provide support and maintain security.

3. Payments

Payments may be handled by ToyyibPay. The payment provider's privacy policy applies to information submitted directly to it. Suplex Gym may retain relevant transaction and payment status records.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Necessary information may be shared with service providers supporting hosting, databases, payment processing, gym access and notifications, or when required by law.

5. Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information. Information is retained as reasonably necessary for memberships, entry records, payments, security and legal obligations.

6. Your Requests

You may contact the gym operator to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Please contact the gym operator if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

8. Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Suplex Gym operator using the gym's official contact details.
